# Reserve 1.6.3

The dashboard keeps its layout and fits your screen as you switch providers,
receive new readings, or change displays.

## A steadier dashboard

- Fixed a gray band that could appear above the dashboard, and a thin strip
  along its right edge, after selecting a provider or when new readings arrived
  while it was open.
- When provider details are taller than the screen allows, only the details
  scroll. The overview and footer stay in place.
- The dashboard sizes itself to the display that shows Reserve's menu bar icon.
- A newly selected provider's details open at the top. Refreshing keeps your
  scroll position.
- An open dashboard resizes when you connect or disconnect a display or change
  its resolution.
