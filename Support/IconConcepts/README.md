# Reserve icon exploration

These concepts were generated with the built-in image generation tool. Concept G is now the production artwork at `Support/Reserve-AppIcon.png`; the other concepts remain exploratory.

All prompts shared these constraints: a premium contemporary macOS icon, matte near-black tile, warm-ivory primary geometry, one coral reserve element, flat vector-like execution, no text or provider logos, no skeuomorphism, and clear recognition at 16 px.

## A — Cut ring

Pure-geometry capacity mark: one heavy open ring, a detached coral segment, and an inward radial cut. Intended to express available capacity without a letterform. Rejected as too close to a speedometer or dial.

## B — Negative-space monogram

A custom constant-width ribbon forming a subtle `R`, with a coral allocation held inside the upper chamber. Cleaner than the production monogram but still too conventional as a lettermark.

## C — Reserve stack

Three thick horizontal provider slabs with a coral portion separated from the middle slab. Strong product logic but too close to a menu or list icon.

## D — Protected pocket

One continuous ivory ribbon folded into three runs around a protected coral block. The strongest conceptual territory so far, but the soft geometry can read as an `S` or routed cable.

## E — Detached reserve

One large ivory mass with a precisely removed corner and a matching coral piece held aside. The reserve metaphor is immediate and works at small sizes, but the current base silhouette can resemble a photo or mountain tile.

## F — Reserve bay

A harder architectural convergence of D and E: one compact ivory enclosure around a coral modular unit. It is the strongest candidate in this round and remains crisp at 16 px. Its next refinement should reduce the slight lock/vault association while preserving the protected-allocation metaphor.

## G — Fuel dial

A modern fuel gauge with five capacity segments, a coral reserve zone, and a needle indicating remaining range. It communicates the product immediately, survives small sizes, and was selected as the production app icon. The menu bar uses a purpose-built monochrome template companion with the same segmented arc and a live needle.

## H — Fuel tank

A simplified fuel-tank outline holding five capacity cells, including a coral reserve cell. The tank metaphor is distinctive at full size but compresses toward a battery pictogram in the menu bar.

## I — Range arc

A single continuous range arc with a coral reserve end and a dark index cut. More logo-like than G, but the internal cut becomes ambiguous at small sizes.

## J — Modern fuel gauge

A refined continuous range arc with one coral reserve zone and a floating indicator. This is the lead fuel-inspired candidate: the metaphor is immediate, the construction is only three shapes, and the mark remains legible at 16 px.

## Recommended next refinement

Concept G was selected. Keep the full-color segmented gauge for the app icon and the monochrome live-needle companion for the menu bar.
