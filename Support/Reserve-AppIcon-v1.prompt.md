# Reserve app icon generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand

Asset type: premium native macOS app icon, 1024 x 1024 master artwork

Primary request: Create a distinctive app icon for “Reserve”, a private macOS menu-bar utility that monitors remaining AI subscription quotas, reset windows, local token totals, and subscription value across three providers.

Scene/backdrop: The icon itself sits centered on a perfectly flat solid `#00ff00` chroma-key background for later removal. The green background must be uniform edge-to-edge, with no shadows, gradients, texture, floor plane, reflections, or lighting variation. Do not use `#00ff00` anywhere in the icon.

Subject: A single warm-obsidian rounded-square Mac app tile. Inside it, create three precisely nested open gauge arcs or reservoir bands around a small quiet circular core. Each arc has a deliberately open gap, expressing measurable capacity remaining and recurring reset cycles. The geometry should feel balanced and memorable at 16 px, with one clear silhouette—not a dashboard screenshot.

Style/medium: refined vector-like 3D icon, subtle dimensionality, Apple-quality macOS utility icon, restrained and engineered rather than playful.

Composition/framing: centered, generous even padding, orthographic front view, symmetrical overall mass with slightly varied arc lengths. Keep all artwork fully separated from the chroma background.

Lighting/mood: soft controlled studio lighting only within the tile, calm and confident.

Color palette: warm near-black espresso and walnut-brown tile matching `#1B1412` and `#32231D`; three restrained meter accents in warm ivory `#EBE7E3`, burnt orange `#E86638`, and clear sky blue `#5CA8E8`; a tiny muted green `#75C799` status core. Avoid rainbow effects.

Materials/textures: fine satin ceramic or anodized metal, very subtle depth, crisp edges, no glassmorphism.

Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no provider logos, no robot, no brain, no lightning bolt, no currency symbol, no generic sparkle, no chart screenshot. Must remain legible and distinctive at tiny menu-bar/app-icon sizes. No cast shadow outside the tile, no contact shadow, no reflection, no watermark.

Avoid: excessive gradients, neon cyberpunk, glossy candy style, busy detail, photorealistic objects, similarity to a speedometer or battery icon.
