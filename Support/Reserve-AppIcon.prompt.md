# Reserve app icon generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand

Asset type: premium modern 1024 x 1024 macOS app icon for Reserve

Primary request: Create a modern abstract fuel gauge for Reserve, a menu-bar app that tells people how much usable capacity remains across their AI subscriptions, like knowing how much gas remains in the tank.

Subject: A low, wide dashboard fuel gauge built from one bold 210-degree arc, not a full circle. The arc is divided into five clean contiguous capacity segments with narrow even gaps. Four segments are warm ivory; the final low-fuel segment at the far left is coral-orange, clearly the reserve zone. A single thick ivory needle rises from a small center pivot and points toward roughly three-quarters full. Add one tiny abstract droplet-shaped cutout inside the pivot, subtle enough not to clutter the mark. The gauge should communicate remaining range immediately, without letters E or F.

Style/medium: flat vector-like automotive instrument design, contemporary electric-vehicle dashboard restraint, precise geometry, bold enough for 16 px, premium developer-tool identity. No realism.

Composition/framing: centered gauge occupying about 64% of a matte rounded-square tile. Arc in upper-middle, pivot below it, balanced negative space. Strong silhouette.

Color palette: true near-black charcoal background (`#101113`), warm bone ivory gauge and needle (`#F2EEE7`), vivid restrained coral reserve segment (`#FF6842`). Strictly flat; no gradients, glow, glass, or chrome.

Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no fuel-pump illustration, no car, no provider logos, no chart, no robot, no brain, no sparkle, no lightning, no battery. No 3D, bevel, gloss, shadow, texture, leather, plastic, or glass. Must remain clear at 16 px.

Avoid: speedometer with many tick marks, tachometer, circular loading spinner, generic analytics gauge, retro dashboard, warning triangle, flame, watermark.
